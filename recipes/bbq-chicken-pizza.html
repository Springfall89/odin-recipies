BBQ Chicken Pizza

Description

This recipe uses store-bought crust. It's easy to prepare and will leave you with tasty results.

Ingredients

  • 1 (12 inch) pre-baked pizza crust
  • 1 cup spicy barbeque sauce
  • 2 cooked and cubed skinless boneless chicken breast halves
  • 1 cup sliced pepperoncini peppers
  • 1 cup chopped red onion
  • 1/2 cup chopped fresh cilantro
  • 2 cups shredded Colby-Jack cheese

Steps

  1. Gather ingredients and preheat the oven at 350 degrees F (175 degrees C).
  2. Place pizza crust on baking sheet and spread barbeue sauce on it.
  3. Top with chicken, pepperoncini peppers, onion, and cilantro.
  4. Cover with Colby-Jack cheese.
  5. Bake in oven for 15 minutes until the cheese is melted and bubbly.
  6. Serve hot and enjoy!
