Macarons

Description

Macarons are the most delicious soft cookies with crisp edges. This recipe makes them using ground almonds, confectioners' sugar, and egg whites.

Ingredients

  • 3 egg whites
  • 1/4 cup white sugar
  • 1 2/3 cups confectioners' sugar
  • 1 cup finely ground almonds
  • Filling or frosting of your choice

Steps

  1. Line baking sheet with baking mat.
  2. Beat egg whites in a bowl of a mixxer until foamy. Add white sugar and continue beating until the egg whites are glossy, fluffy, and hold soft peaks.
  3. Sift confectioners' sugar and ground almonds in another bowl. Quickly fold almond mixture into egg whites for about 30 strokes.
  4. Spoon the small amount of batter into a plastic bag and cut off the tip to make a piping bag. Pipe a test disk of batter 1 1/2 inches in diameter onto the baking sheet. If the batter holds a peak instead of flattening, continue folding the batter a few more times and retest.
  5. After enough folds and the batter flattens immediately into an even disk, spoon batter into pastry bag with a round tip. Pipe the batter onto the baking sheet in rounds, leaving space between the disks. Leave the piped cookies stand out in room temperature for about 1 hour until they form a hard skin on top.
  6. Preheat the oven to 285 degrees F (140 degrees C)
  7. Bake cookies in oven for about 10 minutes until they are set, but not browned.
  8. Let the cookies cool before adding your choice of filling or frosting on the cookie. Preferably, add your filling or frosting into a pastry bag with a round tip and pipe them onto the cookie for a uniform look. After that, sandwich another cookie on top.
  9. Serve in batches and enjoy!
